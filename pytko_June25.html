Pytko June 2025 Gig

Projection Mapping Audiovisual Art TouchDesigner
Year:Company:Collaborators:
2025 — Julia Pytko Elif Bora

I collaborated with musician Julia Pytko as the visual artist for her prformance at The Greyhound, Peckham, in June 2025. Her tracks are dreamy, slow, and melodic. The visuals were created in TouchDesigner, and Kinect Camera is used for capturing the performers posture and reflecting it on more abstactly on the background, and the visuals were brought to life through projection mapping on stage. The audio input is used as well to create more dynamic and reactive visuals. The shift between these modes was based on the moods of the tracks and controlled manually, mapped to a midi controller.
